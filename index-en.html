Experimentation · A/B Testing

Evaluating the Impact of New Traffic Sources through A/B Experimentation

Statistics A/B Testing Experimentation Producto Negocio

Problem:
A digital product needs to evaluate whether adding new traffic sources improves conversion-funnel performance and, in particular, the key payment event. The goal is to make an informed decision on whether to scale these sources without compromising profitability or user experience.

What do I do in this project?
– Build and analyze the event funnel
– Validate the experimental design using A/A
– Run A/B across multiple funnel events
– Rigorous statistical evaluation (including multiple comparisons)
– Interpret findings and final recommendation

Value added:
I don’t just report metrics: I translate statistical results into actionable decisions (when to scale an initiative, when to stop it, and what additional changes could create more impact). This reduces the risk of investing resources without proven benefit and strengthens evidence-based decision-making.

Key metrics:

  • Evaluation of over 4,900 users distributed in three experimental groups.
  • Behavior analysis across 5 key events conversion funnel events.
  • Observed differences were not statistically significant at α = 0.05 nor under the multiple-testing adjusted level.

Churn Prediction and Customer Segmentation

EDA Machine Learning Clustering business

Problem:

What do I do in this project?

NLP and text classification

Amazon review classification

NLP Classification Machine learning

Problem:
A large volume of Amazon product reviews makes it difficult to prioritize which feedback to review manually. The goal is to train a model that automatically classifies reviews into categories to speed up customer-sentiment understanding and detect potential issues.

What do I do in this project?
– Text cleaning and normalization, tokenization, TF-IDF vectorization, and training of multiclass classification models.
– Performance evaluation using metrics such as accuracy and F1, and interpretation of results to understand the model’s strengths and limitations.

Value added:
The model makes it easier to prioritize reviews and understand the overall tone of customer feedback without reading each text manually. It can be integrated into a product quality monitoring or customer support workflow, helping respond faster to recurring issues and detect dissatisfaction patterns.

Key metrics:

  • Model training with 103,460 reviews and a vocabulary of 51,932 terms distintos.
  • Best model (LinearSVC) with 89.2% accuracy on the test set.
  • Weighted F1-score: 0.88, showing a good balance between precision and coverage across classes.

EDA and customer behavior

Exploratory analysis and customer behavior segmentation

EDA Segmentation Purchase behavior

Problem:
An online retailer needs to better understand how, when, and what customers buy in order to optimize assortment, promotions, and logistics. The goal is to explore orders and products data to detect demand patterns and behavioral segments.

What do I do in this project?
– Explore and describe user, order, and product data.
– Preprocessing, duplicate removal, and creation of frequency, recency, and monetary metrics.
– Identify purchase patterns and customer segments based on the generated metrics and visualizations.

Value added:
The analysis helps answer questions such as how loyal customers are, what times they buy the most, and which demand patterns repeat. It can be used to design segmented campaigns, adjust inventory, and prioritize improvements in the shopping experience.

Key analysis metrics:

  • Customers place an average of 3.4 orders per month, with a recurring segment that exceeds 6 orders per month.
  • 35% of orders occur between 10:00 and 15:00, identifying the most relevant operational window for demand.
  • RFM segmentation shows that only 18% of users generate 42% of all purchases, highlighting a high-value segment.
